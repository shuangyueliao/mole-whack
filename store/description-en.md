# Mole Whack — Store Submission Materials

## Short description (CrazyGames / GameDistribution / itch.io)

Whack goofy moles popping from a 3x3 meadow for 30 seconds. Chain hits for
combo multipliers, grab golden moles for triple points, and dodge the bombs.
Rank up from F to S!

(168 characters)

## Details

- Genre: Arcade / Casual / Reflex
- Controls: Mouse / Touch — tap or click a mole to whack it. Keyboard:
  Enter or Space to start.
- Features: 30-second timed rounds, 3x3 hole grid, combo multiplier
  (1 + min(combo,10) x 0.1, up to x2), golden moles (+3), bombs (-5 and
  combo break), mole stay-time shrinks from 900ms to 450ms, F/D/C/B/A/S
  rating, best score + best rank saved locally, mute toggle, hammer swing
  and star-particle effects, procedural graphics & sounds, single-file
  HTML5 (no external assets), works offline once loaded.
- Tech: Vanilla JavaScript, Canvas, WebAudio. No frameworks, no tracking.
